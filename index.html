Blue Letter Bible Suite Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Blue Letter Bible Suite is a Chrome extension developed by Arun Amalraj.

Contact: arunamalrajmca@gmail.com

1. Overview

Blue Letter Bible Suite ("BLB Suite", "the extension", "we", or "our") provides Bible study tools for use with the Blue Letter Bible website and related Bible-study workflows.

This privacy policy explains what information the extension processes, where that information is stored, and when information may be sent to external websites.

2. Information stored by the extension

BLB Suite uses Chrome's local extension storage to save information needed for its features and user preferences.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • Extension preferences and settings.
  • Study-session information.
  • Search terms and Bible references associated with study activity.
  • User-created notes and related study information.
  • Feature and interface preferences.

This information is stored using Chrome's extension storage and is not transmitted to the developer as an analytics or telemetry service.

3. Bible-page and webpage processing

Some BLB Suite features operate on webpages in the browser.

Depending on the feature being used, the extension may inspect webpage text, Bible references, selections, or other page information needed to provide functions such as:

  • Bible reference detection.
  • Double-click Bible reference handling.
  • Show-on-Blue-Letter-Bible functionality.
  • Search and study tools.
  • Context-menu actions.
  • Other Bible-study features provided by the extension.

The extension does not use this access to create an advertising profile of the user.

4. Case-sensitive KJV search

The b cs case-sensitive KJV search uses a bundled original-case King James Version corpus.

Case-sensitive matching is performed locally within the extension.

The extension does not need to send the complete KJV corpus to an external service to perform this matching.

When matching references are subsequently opened in Blue Letter Bible's native search or MultiVerse pages, the selected search query or Bible references may be sent to the Blue Letter Bible website as part of that navigation.

5. Searches sent to external websites

BLB Suite can navigate the browser to external websites, including Blue Letter Bible, when the user requests Bible searches, references, or related features.

For example, a search entered through the extension's b or b cs commands may result in navigation to Blue Letter Bible.

When a search is intentionally sent to an external website, that website receives the information necessary to process the requested search or page navigation.

Blue Letter Bible's handling of that information is governed by Blue Letter Bible's own privacy practices and policies, not this policy.

6. Data sharing

BLB Suite does not sell personal information.

BLB Suite does not operate an advertising or behavioral-tracking service.

The extension does not intentionally transmit stored study notes, study history, or preferences to the developer.

Information may be sent to an external website when the user explicitly uses a feature that requires navigation or searching on that website.

7. Analytics and tracking

BLB Suite does not include a separate developer-operated analytics or telemetry service.

The extension does not intentionally collect browsing history for advertising or profiling purposes.

8. Third-party websites

BLB Suite can interact with or navigate to third-party websites, particularly Blue Letter Bible, when the user requests Bible-study functionality.

Those websites operate independently and may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Users should review the privacy policies of third-party websites they choose to visit.

9. Data security

BLB Suite uses Chrome's extension storage mechanisms and the browser's standard security model for locally stored extension data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely guaranteed to be secure. Users should keep their browser and operating system updated and use appropriate account-security practices.

10. Children's privacy

BLB Suite is a general Bible-study extension and is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children.

The extension does not intentionally collect children's personal information as a separate category.

11. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension's data practices or relevant requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
